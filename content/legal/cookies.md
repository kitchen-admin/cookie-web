## Overview

This Cookie Policy explains how Cookie uses cookies, local storage, mobile identifiers, SDKs, pixels, and similar technologies.

These technologies help us operate secure sessions, remember preferences, support analytics, deliver reminders, understand product performance, and improve Cookie. They may be used on our website, web views, mobile apps, emails, and related services.

## Technologies We May Use

| Type | Examples | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strictly necessary | Session cookies, authentication tokens, fraud-prevention signals, load-balancing identifiers. | Operate the service, keep you signed in, secure accounts, prevent abuse, and provide requested features. |
| Preference | Theme selection, language, region, cookie preference, notification settings. | Remember choices and make Cookie feel consistent across visits. |
| Analytics and diagnostics | Page views, feature usage, crash data, performance metrics, anonymous device identifiers. | Understand reliability, improve design, fix bugs, and measure aggregate product health. |
| Local app storage | Cached recipes, pantry state, reminder settings, personalization signals, offline data. | Enable fast, local, and offline-friendly cooking experiences. |
| Mobile identifiers | App instance identifiers, push tokens, device analytics identifiers, platform advertising controls where enabled. | Deliver notifications, measure app health, prevent fraud, and honor device choices. |

## Consent and Preference Management

Where law requires consent for optional cookies, analytics, local storage, or similar technologies, Cookie will request consent or provide controls before using those technologies for the relevant purpose. Necessary technologies may operate without consent when required to provide the service.

You can manage browser cookies through your browser settings. Mobile operating systems may provide controls for tracking, analytics, notifications, advertising identifiers, and app permissions. Cookie will respect legally required consent, withdrawal, and opt-out choices where applicable.

## Analytics

Analytics help us understand aggregate product health, performance, feature usage, onboarding quality, and reliability. We use analytics to improve Cookie, not to sell personal information or provide advertisers access to personal data.

Analytics vendors and tools may change over time. We prefer privacy-conscious configurations, data minimization, retention limits, and aggregation where practical.

## Retention

Cookie and local storage durations vary by purpose. Session technologies may expire when your session ends. Preference storage may remain until you clear it, change preferences, or the storage expires. Analytics and diagnostics retention depends on tool settings, legal requirements, and operational needs.

## Managing Your Choices

- Use browser settings to block or delete cookies and local storage.
- Use device settings to manage notifications, analytics, tracking permissions, and app identifiers.
- Use Cookie settings, where available, to adjust preferences, notifications, and optional processing.
- Email [support@cookie.kitchen](mailto:support@cookie.kitchen) for privacy requests related to cookies or identifiers.

Some browsers or regions support global privacy signals. Where legally required and technically feasible, Cookie will honor recognized opt-out signals for the relevant processing.
